rclone (personal use)

This Google OAuth app is used only by its owner, to transfer files between their own Google Drive and their own server with rclone. It is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy policy

The app collects no personal data and shares nothing with third parties. The Google access token is stored only on the owner's server and is used only to read and write the owner's own Drive files. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
